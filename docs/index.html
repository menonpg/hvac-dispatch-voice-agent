How a call flows through Riley
Same pattern used across our live voice-agent deployments (matchmaking, pharmacy, automotive) — repurposed here for HVAC dispatch.
1

Inbound Call

Customer calls your existing business number — Riley answers in your company's name.

2

Triage

Riley asks what's wrong, flags true emergencies (no heat, gas smell, CO) for immediate escalation.

3

Book & Dispatch

Collects name, address, issue, and preferred window — creates a work order directly in your CRM.

4

Confirm & Log

Customer gets a confirmation; your team sees the call, transcript, and work order on the dashboard instantly.

What Riley handles
🛠️

New Service Requests

Collects customer info, address, issue description, urgency, and preferred appointment window — creates the work order automatically.

📋

Status Checks

"Where's my technician?" — Riley looks up the work order by ID or phone number and gives a status update.

🚨

Emergency Triage

No heat, no AC, gas smell, carbon monoxide, flooding — Riley recognizes true emergencies and escalates immediately to an on-call line.

📅

Scheduling

Checks real-time availability and offers the next open service windows, pulling live from your dispatch calendar.

💬

General Questions

Service area, hours, pricing ranges, maintenance plans — answered straight from your company's knowledge base.

🔁

Multi-Location Ready

One platform, multiple phone numbers — each location or franchise gets its own knowledge base and dispatch backend automatically.
